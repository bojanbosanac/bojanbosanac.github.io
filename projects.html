ParkingNS

Pay for street parking in Novi Sad by SMS — see which zones are nearby, pick your plate, and send in one tap.

Kotlin Jetpack Compose Coroutines & Flow Room Retrofit AI-assisted (Claude Code)
Problem

Paying for street parking in Novi Sad means knowing which zone you're standing in, remembering that zone's SMS number, and re-typing your license plate every time — with no official app to make it easier.

Approach

Built end to end in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with an MVI-style architecture. All eight city zones, one-tap SMS (with a fix for Samsung's Messages app), and live GPS distance to nearby zones from 309 mapped street segments — a zone map that refreshes itself from a versioned remote dataset, so new streets ship without an app update. Developed with an AI-assisted workflow using Claude Code: I defined the architecture, conventions and features, and reviewed and shipped every change.

Outcome

Shipped to Google Play as Parking Novi Sad (v1.1), a free, ad-supported app in Serbian and English. There's no backend or account: plates and location never leave the device, and no card details or registration are needed to pay.
